# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-05
First public release.

- Windows engine (PowerShell 5.1, no dependencies) and Ubuntu/Linux engine (Python 3 stdlib)
- Shared UI with six themes: Cyber, Aero, Liquid Glass, Green Rain, Retro 95, Liquid Metal
- Quick Fix eye: follows the cursor, blinks, and cycles sacred geometry while scanning
- Startup Optimizer, Turbo Boost, Junk Clean, Privacy Sweep, Browser Extensions,
  Shortcut/Launcher Fix, Drivers & GPU, Hardware Health, Disk Check, System Optimization
- Desktop/Start-menu shortcut creator (Windows) and app-menu installer (Linux)
